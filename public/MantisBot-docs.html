Overview

MantisBot was our robot during the INTOTHEDEEP season (2024-2025), representing the latest in our robotics engineering capabilities. This robot showcases cutting-edge computer vision technology and intelligent mechanical design to achieve autonomous pickup and placement with minimal human intervention.

Key Innovation

Camera-Based Differential Claw System: MantisBot features an innovative differential claw mechanism controlled by computer vision. The robot uses a camera to detect objects and precisely pick them up with minimal human intervention, enabling highly accurate and efficient autonomous play.
